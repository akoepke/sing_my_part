Headphones give the best results with play-along; without them the app ignores sounds that match the other parts, which helps but is not perfect. With Bluetooth headphones such as AirPods, choose your computer's built-in microphone above: using the headphones' own mic switches them to low-quality call audio, which sounds distorted. The level bar next to Listening shows whether the mic hears you.

or open

Choose a MusicXML file (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl, or a .zip containing one), then pick your part and the bars you want to sing. The full score is shown a page at a time, the note you should be singing is highlighted in blue, and each of your notes turns green, amber, or red as you sing it.

MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, and Dorico can all export MusicXML. CPDL (cpdl.org) and IMSLP have free choral editions, many with MusicXML downloads.
